# RavenDKP User Guide

## What This Does

RavenDKP helps you bid on items during raids using your DKP points. It automatically pops up when an auction starts and lets you bid quickly with buttons.

## Getting Started

### Installation

1. Put the RavenDKP folder in your World of Warcraft `Interface/AddOns` folder
2. Restart your game

### Your DKP Points

Your DKP points are stored in your guild note. The addon automatically reads this and shows your current balance in the window.

## Using the Addon

### During a Raid

When your raid leader starts an auction, the window will automatically open and show you:
- The item being auctioned
- How much time is left
- The current highest bid
- Your available DKP points

### How to Bid

The window has bid buttons that work like this:

**Main Spec Bids (MS)**
- Click **+10 MS** - Adds 10 DKP to your bid
- Click **+50 MS** - Adds 50 DKP to your bid
- Click **+100 MS** - Adds 100 DKP to your bid
- Click **All In MS** - Bids all your DKP points

**Off-Spec Bids (OS)**
- Click **+10 OS** - Adds 10 DKP for off-spec
- Click **+50 OS** - Adds 50 DKP for off-spec
- Click **+100 OS** - Adds 100 DKP for off-spec
- Click **All In OS** - Bids all your DKP for off-spec

### Bidding Rules

- Minimum bid is 10 DKP
- Each new bid must be at least 10 DKP higher than the current bid
- You cannot bid OS when the current bid is MS
- You cannot bid more DKP than you have

### Opening and Closing the Window

- Click the raven icon on your minimap to open or close the auction window
- The window also opens automatically when an auction starts
- The window closes automatically when an auction ends

### What the Timer Means

- Green bar with "Auction started" - Bidding is open
- Yellow bar with "Auction paused" - Bidding is paused
- Red bar with "Auction closed" - Bidding has ended
- The bar shrinks as time runs out

### Clicking the Raven

Click on the animated raven in the window for a fun surprise!

## Tips

- Watch the timer bar so you don't miss out
- Your bid is sent to raid chat so everyone can see it
- If you're the highest bidder, your name appears in green
- You can only bid higher than the current bid, not match it (unless you're already the highest bidder)

## What You Need

- A guild that uses the SotA DKP system
- DKP points in your guild note (formatted like `<50>` for 50 DKP)
- To be in a raid group
